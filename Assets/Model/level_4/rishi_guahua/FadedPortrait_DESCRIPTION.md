# FadedPortrait — Wall Hanging Painting

## Description
A decorative framed wall painting for a dark, industrial/abandoned environment.
This variant features an ornate gilded frame, heavily corroded and tarnished with age,
holding a portrait painting barely visible beneath layers of grime, dust, and yellowed varnish.
The elaborate baroque-style frame has peeling gold leaf — perfect for post-apocalyptic or
abandoned building interior decoration.

## Art Direction
- Style: Dark, moody, industrial/abandoned aesthetic
- Frame: Ornate gilded, corroded and tarnished, peeling gold leaf
- Canvas: Portrait subject obscured by grime, dust, yellowed varnish
- Condition: Heavily aged, post-apocalyptic decay
