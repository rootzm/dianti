# AbstractDecay — Wall Hanging Painting

## Description
A decorative framed wall painting for a dark, industrial/abandoned environment.
This variant features a weathered, cracked wooden frame with splintered and missing chunks,
holding an abstract expressionist painting showing heavy water damage stains, brown mold patches,
and peeling canvas — maximum environmental storytelling for abandoned building interiors.

## Art Direction
- Style: Dark, weathered, post-apocalyptic abandoned aesthetic
- Frame: Rough splintered wood with missing chunks, extreme weathering
- Canvas: Abstract shapes beneath water damage, brown mold patches, peeling canvas
- Condition: Maximum decay — water damaged, molded, structurally compromised
